HobbyFi Copilot Facility Rules & Refund Policy

1. Cancellation Policy
- Users can cancel their bookings up to 24 hours in advance for a full refund.
- Cancellations made within 24 hours of the booking slot are not eligible for a refund.
- Memberships can be cancelled at any time, but the current month will not be refunded.

2. Facility Rules
- All players must wear non-marking shoes on the badminton courts.
- Yoga mats are provided, but users are encouraged to bring their own.
- No food or drinks (except water) allowed on the playing surfaces.
- Membership trials are strictly limited to one per user per year.

3. Extensions
- Memberships cannot be paused.
- In case of medical emergencies (with a valid doctor's note), a membership can be frozen for up to 30 days.
